2 · Counter treatment — adapts to the ground

The counter is the one thing that changes between themes — the brand color does not. On dark, a knockout is a genuine hole, so a wallpaper shows through the M and the disc reads at 9.37:1. On light the same hole gives 1.67:1 and the mark dissolves, so the counter fills with --brand-ink (7.95:1) and the mark carries its own structure. Geometry is identical in both.

4 · Rubik at the app's real sizes
